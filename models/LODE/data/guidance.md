### 1.2 The one piece of true hard guidance: H2 2026

> "Our solar recycling facility will be up and running in August and operate **at least 25% of rated capacity** from then through year end, if not higher, generating an expected **$5 million in revenues in the second half of 2026**." — Corrado De Gasperis, [Q2 2026 PR, 2026-07-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1120970/000143774926024415/ex_992178.htm)

- **[HARD]** $5.0M Metals revenue, H2 2026 (Jul–Dec), at ≥25% of 100,000 t/yr rated capacity.
- **[HARD]** Three stated H2 milestones: "full commissioning, **profitable unit economics**, and ramp up" ([Q2 2026 PR, 2026-07-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1120970/000143774926024415/ex_992178.htm)).
- Management explicitly **refused to guide above 25%**: "We're not going to guide past running this 25% level… It's just still too nascent for us to be able to protect the ramp up. We've got incentive to double that number." ([Q2 2026 call, 2026-07-23](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lode/transcripts/662737-q2-2026/))
- 25% = **2,000–2,083 tons/month**; 50% = **4,000 tons/month**. Those are the two stated "intermediate objectives," after which "we'll update our guidance" ([Q2 2026 call, 2026-07-23](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lode/transcripts/662737-q2-2026/)).

**Timing slipped after the Q2 call.** The [2026-08-11 PR](https://comstock.inc/press-release/comstock-metals-brings-fully-integrated-industry-scale-solar-recycling-system-online/) states final integration completes **mid-August**, with the ramp beginning immediately after, and: "We anticipate reaching our **first production milestone in September**" — i.e. continuous operation at 25% of rated capacity in **September**, not August. Operations move from 24h/5-day to **24/7 on 12-hour rotating shifts**.

**[DERIVED] Reconciling the $5M guide:**

| Scenario | Months at 25% | Tons | Implied revenue/ton |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aug–Dec (original Q2 PR framing) | 5 | ~10,400 | **~$480/t** |
| Sep–Dec (post-Aug 11 framing) | 4 | ~8,300 | **~$600/t** |
| At management's stated $750/t gross economics | 4 | ~8,300 | $6.25M (so $5M ≈ **80% achieved**) |

So the $5M guide is roughly internally consistent with a **September start at slightly below the full $750/ton** economics — it is *not* consistent with 25% utilization for a full five months at full unit economics. Treat $5M as the conservative anchor.

### 1.3 Revenue model mechanics — the two (really four) revenue streams

Reported revenue is disaggregated in the 10-Q into four lines. Q2 2026 actuals ([10-Q Note 15, 2026-07-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1120970/000143774926024304/lode20260630_10q.htm)):

| Stream | Q2 2026 | H1 2026 | H1 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recycling (tipping fees recognized) | $65,602 | $106,489 | $0 |
| Decommissioning services | $131,506 | $359,889 | $1,040,701 |
| Off-take (aluminum, glass, tailings) | $37,966 | $43,902 | $11,060 |
| **Metals segment total** | **$235,074** | **$510,280** | **$1,051,761** |
| Mining & real estate lease (other segments) | $37,750 | $76,000 | $73,600 |
| **Consolidated revenue** | **$272,824** | **$586,280** | **$1,125,361** |

**Stated per-ton unit economics** (most explicit version, [Q2 2025 call, 2025-08-14](https://www.insidermonkey.com/blog/comstock-inc-amexlode-q2-2025-earnings-call-transcript-1591840/)):

| Component | Per ton of panels received | Class |
|---|---|---|
| **Tipping fee** (customer pays Comstock to take the panel) | **~$500/t** (≈ $10/panel) | [TARGET] / realized in demo |
| Off-take — aluminum | **~$125/t** | [TARGET] |
| Off-take — silver-rich tailings | **~$125/t** (at ~$25–30/oz silver) | [TARGET] |
| Off-take — glass | **~$20/t** (management elsewhere: "$20, $30, $40 a ton") | [TARGET] |
| **Total off-take** | **~$250/t** ("slightly over $250") | [TARGET] |
| **Total revenue per ton** | **~$750/t** | [TARGET] |
| Totally variable cost (natural gas + electricity ≈ 92–93% of variable) | **~$35/t (~7% of revenue)** | [TARGET] |
| **All-in cost per ton** (incl. fixed: labor, maintenance, S&M, logistics, admin) | **~$150/t** | [TARGET] |

Earlier version of the same math used **~$200/t all-in** and "~75%, you could push 80% operating margin" ([Q4 2024 call, 2025-03-06](https://www.insidermonkey.com/blog/comstock-inc-amexlode-q4-2024-earnings-call-transcript-1476254/)).

**Silver-price leverage on the off-take line** — this is the single biggest swing factor:

> "even at **$60 silver**, our offtake revenue isn't $125 a ton. **It's $375 a ton**." — [FY2025 call, 2026-03-24](https://www.roic.ai/quote/LODE/transcripts/2025-year/4-quarter)

- At ~$60/oz silver, tailings off-take ≈ **$375/t** vs ~$125/t at ~$25–30/oz → total revenue/ton ≈ **$1,000/t** rather than $750/t. **[TARGET]**
- Critically, that $125/$375 "reflects us capturing **only 50% or 60% of the silver value**" because tailings are sold to a third-party refiner, leaving silicon metal, copper, gallium/tellurium/indium behind ([FY2025 call, 2026-03-24](https://www.roic.ai/quote/LODE/transcripts/2025-year/4-quarter); [Q2 2025 call, 2025-08-14](https://www.insidermonkey.com/blog/comstock-inc-amexlode-q2-2025-earnings-call-transcript-1591840/)).
- Target with in-house recovery: **>90% silver recovery** ([Q1 2026 call, 2026-05-07](https://www.roic.ai/quote/LODE/transcripts/2026-year/1-quarter)). At that point "our metal recoveries start looking just as good, if not better, than our tipping fees" — i.e. off-take could reach parity with the ~$500/t tipping fee. **[TARGET]**
- Silver price as of the Q2 2026 call: "We're **below $60** now" (down from a $110 peak) ([Q2 2026 call, 2026-07-23](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lode/transcripts/662737-q2-2026/)). Management declined to forecast silver.
- Glass has been repriced upward by demand from fiber-optic and cement customers; product-upgrade (Eddy) capex was added specifically to hit "high-specification" glass specs ([Q1 2026 call, 2026-05-07](https://www.roic.ai/quote/LODE/transcripts/2026-year/1-quarter)). Glass does not travel far economically at $20–40/t.

### 1.4 Revenue **recognition** vs **billings** — essential modeling mechanic

Per [10-Q Note 13, 2026-07-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1120970/000143774926024304/lode20260630_10q.htm):

> "Recycling service revenue is **deferred upon receipt** of the photovoltaic panels from the customers and **recognized upon completion of the services and the issuance of a certificate of destruction**."

| Deferred revenue (Comstock Metals) | Amount |
|---|---|
| Balance 2024-12-31 | $151,939 |
| Billed during FY2025 | $2,811,984 |
| Recognized during FY2025 | $(864,072) |
| Balance 2025-12-31 | $2,099,851 |
| Billed during H1 2026 | $1,852,514 |
| Recognized during H1 2026 | $(105,858) |
| **Balance 2026-06-30** | **$3,846,507** |

- H1 2026 total Metals **billings $2,309,422** = $510,280 recognized + $1,799,142 deferred ([10-Q, 2026-07-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1120970/000143774926024304/lode20260630_10q.htm)).
- FY2025: revenue **$1.4M**, total billings **$3.5M** ("just as we guided to") ([FY2025 PR, 2026-03-24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1120970/000143774926009779/ex_936614.htm); [FY2025 call, 2026-03-24](https://www.roic.ai/quote/LODE/transcripts/2025-year/4-quarter)). FY2024 revenue $0.4M.
- Panels on the ground awaiting processing: **~6,000+ tons** (May 2026), **"approach 9,000 tons"** / "8,000–9,000 tons" (July 2026), across Nevada, California and Ohio sites ([Q1 2026 call, 2026-05-07](https://www.roic.ai/quote/LODE/transcripts/2026-year/1-quarter); [Q2 2026 call, 2026-07-23](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/lode/transcripts/662737-q2-2026/)).
- **[DERIVED]** $3.85M deferred ÷ ~9,000 tons ≈ **$427/ton** of already-billed tipping fee sitting in the backlog — a rough floor cross-check on the ~$500/t tipping fee (some stockpiled tons are not yet billed, so the true realized fee is probably higher).
- **Modeling consequence:** a large share of the H2 2026 $5M is the *release of the existing $3.85M deferred balance* as stockpiled panels are processed and certificates of destruction issue — not new billings. Model deferred revenue as an explicit balance-sheet roll-forward, not as a plug.

### 4.3 Reconciling "$20.0M" vs "over $45.0M"

**There is no actual conflict — the $20.0M in the 10-Q is the cash Initial Payment line item, not a statement of total consideration.** `$20,000,000` appears in the Q2 10-Q exactly twice, both times as "**$20,000,000 in cash (the 'Initial Payment')**", the first of five enumerated bullets in the purchase-price list. The 10-Q never totals the consideration. The earnings release totals it; the 10-Q itemises it.

**[DERIVED] bridge from $20.0M to $45.0M:**

| Component | $M | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| Initial Payment (cash) | 20.0 | 20.0 |
| 2,000,000 Mackay Parent shares at closing value | 4.5 | 24.5 |
| Second Tranche Payment | 7.0 | 31.5 |
| Contingent Payment | 10.0 | 41.5 |
| 1.5% NSR royalty, carried at its buyout price | 3.5 | **45.0** |
| *(memo) reclamation liability assumed by Mackay* | *6.7* | *51.7 gross* |
| *(memo) less reclamation bond deposit surrendered* | *(4.3)* | *47.4 net* |

**$20.0 + 4.5 + 7.0 + 10.0 + 3.5 = exactly $45.0M**, which is why the release says "**more than** $45.0 million" — the excess is the reclamation relief. Comstock has never published this bridge, so treat the composition as derived, not as guidance. The company's own June framing was consistent: "total consideration of over $45.0 million, **including $20.0 million in immediate cash proceeds expected in August 2026**" ([Q2 2026 PR, 2026-07-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1120970/000143774926024415/ex_992178.htm)) — the $20.0M is explicitly a *subset* of the $45.0M.

**For modelling, the $45.0M headline is not $45.0M of value to Comstock.** Risk-weighted:

| Bucket | $M | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| **Hard, received** | **24.5** | $20.0M cash + $4.5M in MACK stock (mark-to-market, thinly traded) |
| **Hard, receivable** | **7.0** | secured by Deed of Trust, ≤$2.0M may come as stock, due ~Feb 2028 |
| **Optional / contingent** | **13.5** | $10.0M contingent (requires a construction decision or a ≥$500M change of control) + $3.5M NSR buyout, at Mackay's option |

Reasonable base case: **$31.5M** of realisable consideration, $24.5M of it already in hand. Use **$45.0M only as the high case.**

### 7.2 Cross-checks between levers

- **Levers 1 and 2 multiply.** At the default ($150M × 47.63%) Comstock's SSOF stake is worth **$71.4M**, against a **$49.0M carrying value** and **$37.3M of cash invested**. At the low ($76M × 47.63% = $36.3M) the stake is worth **less than book** — a plausible impairment trigger worth modelling. At the high ($300M × 47.63% = $143M) it is roughly **2.9x book**.
- **Levers 3 and 4 must stay consistent.** Because lever 3 is $0, the model should carry SSOF at a *valuation*, not as cash, until the quarter in lever 4. Nothing in any filing supports booking SSOF proceeds in 2026.
- **Levers 10 and 15 overlap.** The $20.0M in lever 10 is already inside the $43M of lever 8/15. Count it once.
- **Levers 12 and 13 interact.** The $1.5M/yr mining saving lands partly in the Mining segment and partly in shared corporate functions. If the model already removes the Mining segment wholesale, do **not** also cut Corporate cash G&A by $1.5M/yr.
- **Levers 6, 7 and 15.** If Bioleum's third-party raise lands (management: "before the end of this year," [TARGET]), lever 6 stays $0 and consolidated burn falls by roughly **$3.6M/quarter** — which is the single largest swing factor in the cash bridge, larger than the entire mining-cost saving.
- **Lever 13 vs §1.5.** Management's claim that facility #1 at 40–50% utilisation covers *all* corporate cost implies a total post-Mining-sale cost structure of roughly **$25–30M/yr**. Corporate cash G&A of $2.25M/qtr ($9M/yr) plus Bioleum at $14.5M/yr plus Metals fixed cost is consistent with that. The levers hang together.
