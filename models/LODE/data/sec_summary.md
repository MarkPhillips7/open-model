## Accounting quirks and things that will bite a modeller

1. **Segment structure is five segments reported in two tiers.** The segment note first totals Metals + Mining + Strategic Investments + Corporate/Other into a "Total of Segments before Bioleum Corp." column, then adds Bioleum to reach consolidated. Segments are Metals, Mining, Strategic Investments, Corporate / Other, Bioleum (Fuels). Bioleum has a different chief operating decision maker (the President of Bioleum) from the rest of the company, which is why it is broken out separately rather than aggregated.
2. **The legacy mining sale is held for sale, not discontinued operations.** Because the disposal is held for sale rather than discontinued operations, NO prior period is restated and Mining segment revenue stays inside continuing-operations revenue in every quarter of this file. Mining revenue is only about $33k per quarter (real-estate and mineral-claim leases) so the revenue that disappears on closing is immaterial; what changes materially is the balance sheet (roughly $23.0m of assets and $6.8m of liabilities, including the reclamation liability, leave the balance sheet) plus about $0.6m per quarter of Mining segment G&A, and a gain or loss on sale lands in the quarter of closing (expected 2026 Q3).
3. **Revenue types do not include any technology, licensing or service line.** The only four revenue types LODE discloses are Mining and Real Estate, Recycling, Decommissioning Services and Off-take. Essentially all revenue is Metals-segment decommissioning and off-take plus roughly $33k–$40k a quarter of mining/real-estate lease income. Bioleum has generated no revenue in any quarter shown.
4. **Gross margin is deeply negative and getting worse, because Comstock Metals is pre-commercial.** Cost of goods sold was reported as $0 for the three reported quarters of 2024 and first appears at $451,938 in the FY2024 10-K, all of which therefore falls in Q4 2024. From 2025 Q1 onward cost of goods sold exceeds revenue every single quarter — $1,168,897 of COGS on $272,824 of revenue in 2026 Q2. The Silver Springs facility was still commissioning through Q2 2026, so these are start-up costs running through COGS rather than a steady-state margin.
5. **The $16.4 million Q2 2026 Flux Photon write-off is split across three lines, two of them inside operating expenses and one below the line.** Flux Photon and related technologies written off in Bioleum: $13,648,042 of developed-technology intangibles, $1,736,481 of capitalized engineering costs for plant designs (in PP&E not yet placed in service), and $1,031,371 of associated investments (below the operating line, in gain_loss_on_investments). Total approximately $16.4 million ($16,415,894), described by the company as substantially all non-cash acquisition costs.
6. **There was a 1-for-10 reverse stock split on 2025-02-24, and the XBRL is inconsistent about it.** EPS and weighted-average share counts for 2024 quarters are restated post-split in the 2025 comparative columns, but the 2024 Q1–Q3 period-end share counts only ever existed in the original 2024 10-Qs and are therefore pre-split (122.6m, 157.8m, 206.6m). Dividing by ten gives the continuous series; the JSON carries both.
7. **LODE mis-tags the context date of prior-year-end comparative balance-sheet columns.** The Q1 2025 10-Q tags 2024-12-31 total assets of $91,309,881 against a 2024-03-31 instant, the Q2 2025 10-Q repeats the error against 2024-06-30, and the Q1 2026 10-Q tags the 2025-12-31 balance sheet against a 2025-03-31 instant. Anyone pulling balance-sheet history from companyfacts or from the latest filing will silently get wrong numbers. Every balance-sheet figure here comes only from the filing whose own report date is that balance-sheet date.
8. **Selling & marketing and general & administrative are not on the face of the income statement.** A single SG&A line is presented. The split only exists in the segment note, which is where the two component rows in this file come from. There is no exploration or pre-development expense line at all in 2024–2026; those costs sit inside SG&A and R&D.
9. **Equity-method affiliates are almost entirely SSOF, and the roster changed sharply in 2026.** Sierra Springs Opportunity Fund went from 16.99% and $20,225,000 at 2025-12-31 to 47.63% and $49,011,855 at 2026-06-30, funded by converting $15,150,000 of advances plus $237,415 of accrued interest into equity and $13,640,000 of new cash. A 40%-held research and development company carried at $1,079,371 was written to zero in Q2 2026 (that is the $1,031,371 loss on investments). Green Li-ion (11.98%, $18,201,065) is carried under the alternative measurement method, not equity method. LINICO is consolidated. GenMat / Quantum Generative Materials is not carried as an equity-method investment in any of these periods.
10. **The income statement is littered with one-time financing items that are not operating.** Losses on conversion of debt ($9.8m in FY2024, $3.3m in 1H2025), losses on debt extinguishment, an $845,000 gain on extinguishment of a liability in 2025 Q1, changes in fair value of derivatives swinging by $1.6m–$2.9m a quarter, a $1,100,000 gain on the Marathon SAFE note in 2026 Q2, and a $403,217 loss on sale of a note receivable. Below-the-line noise is frequently larger than revenue.
11. **Gains on asset disposals are contra-expenses inside operating expenses, not other income.** The $804,489 FY2024 gain, the $200,000 2025 Q2 gain on mineral rights and the $1,400,010 2026 Q1 gain on royalty rights all reduce total operating expenses rather than appearing below the line. In 2026 Q1 that gain is what makes the Mining segment show positive income from operations.
12. **There is no tax line.** No income tax expense or benefit is recognised in any quarter; the company carries a full valuation allowance. Pre-tax loss equals net loss.
13. **Equity issuance is the whole funding story and it is enormous relative to the share count.** Shares outstanding went from 23.5m at 2024-12-31 to 76.0m at 2026-06-30. In 1H 2026 alone LODE raised $60,979,181 of gross common stock proceeds against $4,606,575 of issuance costs. Any forward model needs an explicit ATM/equity-line assumption, not a static share count.
14. **Deferred revenue at Comstock Metals is a real and growing liability.** Current Comstock Metals deferred revenue rose from $2,166,517 at 2025-12-31 to $3,913,173 at 2026-06-30, with $1,746,655 of the 1H 2026 increase flowing through operating cash flow. Recycling customers pay up front for panel decommissioning, so cash receipts lead recognised revenue.
15. **2024 Q4 is distorted by two large non-cash items — do not extrapolate it.** Revenue of $1,599,005 against roughly $425k–$556k in 2024 Q1–Q3 includes $1,562,499 of deferred revenue from the 2023 Mackay Mining Lease recognised on the December 2024 sale of the related mineral interest (Comstock Northern Exploration LLC) to Mackay — which is why current deferred revenue collapses from $1,236,645 at 2024-09-30 to $151,939 at 2024-12-31. R&D of $14,217,336 in the same quarter is almost entirely the $12,244,538 of non-cash R&D recognised on the GenMat transaction. Neither item is cash and neither recurs.
16. **Mackay is a recurring counterparty, not a new one.** Mackay took the 2023 mining lease, bought Comstock Northern Exploration LLC in December 2024, and is the buyer of the entire legacy mining business under the June 2026 Securities Purchase Agreement. The 2026 Q1 $1,400,010 gain on royalty rights and the 2025 Q2 $200,000 gain on mineral rights are part of the same unwinding.
17. **Reported stock-based compensation is unreliable and far too small.** FY2024 stock-based compensation as tagged is $41,799 even though 9M-2024 was $142,482, so the derived 2024 Q4 figure is negative (−$100,683) — that is what the filings say, not an extraction error. More importantly LODE settles large amounts in stock through entirely separate lines: R&D expense paid with common stock ($1,208,180 FY2024, $1,487,476 FY2025), interest expense paid with common stock ($527,541 FY2024, $401,523 FY2025), and $1,864,159 of director share-based compensation in 2026 Q1 alone. Do not use the cash-flow SBC line as a dilution or expense driver.
18. **The derivative liability tag gets reused for something that is not a derivative.** From 2025 Q4 onward LODE tags a balance-sheet caption called "Deferred liabilities" with us-gaap:DerivativeLiabilitiesCurrent ($199,695 at 2025-12-31 and 2026-03-31, $432,065 at 2026-06-30). Over 2026 the actual derivative position is an ASSET of $2,006,187. Treat the derivative liability row as a real derivative only through 2025 Q3.
19. **Line items get re-tagged between filings, and one case would double count $845,000.** The Q1 2025 10-Q put the $845,000 gain on extinguishment of a *liability* into `us-gaap:GainsLossesOnExtinguishmentOfDebt`; later filings correctly use the separate liability tag. Naively reading both would count it twice. Gain on extinguishment of debt for 2025 Q1 is therefore shown as 0 here. Likewise the 2025 Q3 $900,000 SAFE-note remeasurement is tagged `ChangeInFairValueOfCommitments` rather than `ChangeInFairValueOfDebt`.
20. **Investee revenue is tagged in the same element as company revenue.** Revenue facts carrying the EquityMethodInvestmentNonconsolidatedInvesteeAxis dimension are the revenue of the equity-method investees (summarised investee financials), not LODE revenue and not related-party revenue. They are excluded here; including them would double count by up to $1.05m a year.
21. **Quarterly cash flow statements are YTD only.** Discrete-quarter cash flow figures here are derived by subtracting the prior YTD. The as-reported YTD number sits alongside each one in the JSON under a `_ytd` suffix so you can audit the subtraction.
22. **Large non-cash investing and financing activity sits outside the cash flow statement proper.** In 1H 2026: $15,150,000 of SSOF advances converted to equity investment, $9,595,828 of equipment deposits reclassified into PP&E, $6,055,000 of common stock issued for the Flux Photon lease amendment and $2,074,615 for the Flux Photon liability. In FY2025: $12,000,000 of plant and equipment acquired via the Marathon SAFE note and roughly $20.6m of intangibles acquired with stock, derivative liabilities, payables and subsidiary equity. Capex on the cash flow statement badly understates asset additions.
